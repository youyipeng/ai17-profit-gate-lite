# Limitations

Lite is basic triage, not a legal opinion, audited cost model, binding quote or production approval. Complete fields do not independently prove the facts they contain. The script consumes agent- or human-normalized input; it does not parse free text. No autonomous feasibility research, profit estimation, reuse verification, delivery estimation, calibration or deployment is included. Pro figures refer to the separate internal evaluated candidate. Use verified current evidence before business commitments.
